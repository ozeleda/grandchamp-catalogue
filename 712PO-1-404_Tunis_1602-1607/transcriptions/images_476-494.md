# 712PO/1/404 — transcription of images 476–494 (ff. 456v–474v)

Continuation of `images_470-475.md`: the last leaves of the register of the chancery of the
French consulate in Tunis, July–August 1607, written in Italian by the chancellor Hercule
Tamagni.

Image numbers are the "Média" numbers of the online viewer (`images/476.jpg` … `images/494.jpg`).
Each image is a double page: left = verso of the previous folio, right = recto of the folio
numbered in the top corner.

## Conventions and reliability

Same conventions as `images_470-475.md`:

- Line breaks follow the manuscript where possible; spelling is kept; no punctuation is added.
- `p[er]dav[an]ti` = expanded abbreviation; `^…^` = above the line; `~~…~~` = struck through;
  `[…]` = illegible or lost; `(?)` = uncertain reading of the preceding word.
- `▽` = the scudo sign; `s. mon. cunio di Sp[agn]a` = the scribe's coin formula, not resolved
  with certainty.
- This is a working transcription from the scans. The formulaic parts are secure. **Personal
  names, place names and sums are the weakest points** and should be checked against the image
  before being cited. From image 489 onwards the top of every leaf is eaten away, so the first
  lines of each page are lost.
