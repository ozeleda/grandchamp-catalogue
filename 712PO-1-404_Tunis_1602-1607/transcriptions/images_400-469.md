# 712PO/1/404 — transcription of images 400–469 (ff. 382v–450r)

Register of the chancery of the French consulate in Tunis, April – June 1607, written in
Italian by the chancellor Hercule Tamagni. This file precedes `images_470-475.md`.

Image numbers are the "Média" numbers of the online viewer (`images/400.jpg` … `images/469.jpg`).
Each image is a double page: left = verso of the previous folio, right = recto of the folio
numbered in the top corner.

## Conventions and reliability

Same conventions as the other transcription files:

- Line breaks follow the manuscript where possible; spelling is kept; no punctuation is added.
- `p[er]dav[an]ti` = expanded abbreviation; `^…^` = above the line; `~~…~~` = struck through;
  `[…]` = illegible or lost; `(?)` = uncertain reading of the preceding word.
- `▽` = the scudo sign; `s. mon. cunio di Sp[agn]a` = the scribe's coin formula, not resolved
  with certainty.
- This is a working transcription from the scans. The upper third of most leaves is darkened
  by damp and the ink has faded there. The formulaic parts are secure. **Personal names, place
  names and sums are the weakest points** and should be checked against the image before
  being cited.
