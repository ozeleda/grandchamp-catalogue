# 712PO/1/404 — transcription of images 470–475 (ff. 450v–456r)

Register of the chancery of the French consulate in Tunis; acts of 27 June – 14 July 1607
(with one later addition of 17 December 1607), written in Italian by the chancellor
Hercule Tamagni.

Image numbers are the "Média" numbers of the online viewer (`images/470.jpg` … `images/475.jpg`).
Each image is a double page: left = verso of the previous folio, right = recto of the folio
numbered in the top corner.

## Conventions and reliability

- Line breaks follow the manuscript. Spelling and capitalisation are kept; apostrophes and
  punctuation are not added.
- Abbreviations are expanded in square brackets: `p[er]dav[an]ti`, `Canc[ellier]o`.
- `^…^` = written above the line; `~~…~~` = struck through; `[…]` = illegible; `(?)` = uncertain
  reading of the preceding word.
- `▽` stands for the scudo sign used by the scribe. After the amount he writes an abbreviation
  read here as `s. mon. cunio di Sp[agn]a` ("coin of Spain"); the first two elements are not
  resolved with certainty. `a[spri]` renders the sign used for the fraction of the scudo.
- This is a working transcription made from the scans, in a fast, faded hand. The formulaic
  parts are secure. **Personal names, place names and a few sums are the weakest points** and
  are flagged with `(?)`; they should be checked against the image before being cited.
